## Unsupported or Deliberately Excluded Claims

- **“Paper 3 is the first poisoning study in biometrics.”** Unsupported and contradicted by direct face/fingerprint literature.
- **“Guarded EMA introduces the first separate update threshold.”** Unsupported; Giot et al. used separate acceptance and update thresholds in keystroke adaptation.
- **“Selective-update bias” is an established term.** Unsupported; the exact term is introduced by Paper 3 for an authentication-specific mechanism.
- **“HMOG synthetic drift represents natural behavioral drift.”** Unsupported; the transformation is synthetic.
- **“The McGill Day-1/Day-2 difference is exclusively genuine behavioral drift.”** Unsupported; day separation also permits device, context, and acquisition-condition change.
- **“Step-up authentication, multimodal corroboration, or rollback solves accepted-only censoring.”** Untested proposal only.
- **“No prior study jointly evaluates any security and utility dimension.”** Too broad. The defensible claim is that the specific combination of held-out poisoning, temporal recovery, feedback availability, fail-closed branches, and external replication has received limited joint evaluation in the audited literature.
